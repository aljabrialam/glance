    The thesis

    Why now

    Agents are about to shop. Nobody has built the leash.

    Reap's Agentic API gives software a real card at real merchants. The missing piece is permission: who decides that a specific total, with shipping and tax, may become a charge. Today the answer is either "the model" or "the human re-does the checkout".

    What Glance does

    Limit before checkout. Approval on the provider's page. Card never seen.

    A pure, unit-tested rule over whole cents decides whether a checkout may even be opened. The user taps approve on Reap's hosted page, inside the app. The model only ever turns a photo or a sentence into a query and a ceiling.

    Why it bridges onchain and real world

    USDC in a wallet you own pays a merchant that has never heard of crypto.

    The Kwal vault backs the card; Reap routes the purchase; the merchant sees a normal order. Two ways in — Meta Display glasses (concept) or the phone camera (built) — one approval, one honest receipt.
