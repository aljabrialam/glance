How it works
The brain

Glance mobile app

Understands the request and enforces your spending limit before any checkout.

The purchase

Reap Agentic API

Finds the product, prices the order and checks out. You approve every charge.

The money

Kwal USDC vault

Stablecoins in a wallet you own back the card payment at a real merchant.

Built · runs tonight

Phone camera

Point the Glance app at a product or type one sentence. The agent identifies it, prices it, checks your limit, and hands you Reap's approval page.

Concept · this film

Meta Display glasses

The same agent and the same rules, shown as a glance. Approval still happens on your phone. Not built — glasses have no third-party HUD yet.
